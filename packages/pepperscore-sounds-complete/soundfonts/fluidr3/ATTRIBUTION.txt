FluidR3 General MIDI sound samples
Source: https://github.com/gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts/tree/gh-pages/FluidR3_GM
Original soundfont: FluidR3_GM.sf2

https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/

These files are reduced, locally hosted subsets of the MP3 note samples in the
gleitz MIDI.js soundfont collection. Each instrument retains one source sample
near every five semitones in the C2-C7 range. Other pitches are transposed by
the browser player. The source files were generated by the upstream project
from FluidR3_GM.sf2. The packaging transformation is reproducible with
scripts/prepare-fluidr3.mjs.
